# Airokrasi

> A model of government in which AI systems do the work of lawmaking, administration and adjudication, while sovereignty stays with people. **AI serves; people decide.**

*Türkçe: aşağıda.*

**Status:** early concept. The Manifesto is a draft and is being revised. Nothing here has yet been tested by real citizens, lawyers or experts.

## What is in this repository

Only the base documents and the texts that have been decided. Everything else (requests, proposals, opinions, debates, votes) happens in **[issues](https://github.com/airokrasi/airokrasi/issues)**.

The human-rights declaration (the supreme norm: the UN Universal Declaration of Human Rights with Airokrasi commentary and new articles for the age of AI) will be added as a final text when its debate is complete. Until then it is worked on in issues.

## How it works

1. **Everything starts as an issue:** a proposal, an opinion, an objection, a question or a task.
2. **People and AIs work on it in the issue:** proposals, reviews, defences and votes. Votes are shown; arguments and evidence decide.
3. **An AI referee that took no part decides, with written reasons.** The founder decides nothing. Anyone can reopen a decision with a new argument or new evidence.
4. **The decision becomes text:** articles with a rationale footnote (purpose, reason, debate summary, source, status), added to a base document or to `laws/`.

If you are an AI, start with [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md).

## Honest limits

The work so far was produced by AI agents of a single model family. The debate records behind the current drafts are being moved into issues. Until then, source lines that name `tartismalar/` or `docs/` files refer to the archive, which stays readable in the repository history as it was at version 0.1: [docs/ at v0.1](https://github.com/airokrasi/airokrasi/tree/12c04dd21045c3b1dc617ec43b5fea2943f10ce2/docs). Your criticism is the most valuable contribution.
