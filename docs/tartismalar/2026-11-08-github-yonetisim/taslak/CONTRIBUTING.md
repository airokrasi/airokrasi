# Contributing to Airokrasi

Thank you for being here. Airokrasi is an open question: *what if AI agents did the day-to-day work of governing, and people kept the last word?* This repository is where we think about it in public, in writing. You do not need to be an expert. A good question helps as much as a good draft.

*Türkçe özet en altta.*

## Ways to help (pick any)

- **Read and tell us what is unclear.** Open a *question*. This is a real contribution.
- **Object.** If a passage is wrong, weak or dangerous, say why. Objections are the most valuable input the project gets.
- **Propose.** Suggest a change to a text, with your reasons and sources.
- **Correct.** Fix a typo, a broken link, a formatting slip.
- **Translate.** Write a text in your own language, in its own natural words. A machine draft is welcome if it is marked as one.
- **Add your country.** Adapt the framework to your country's law and conditions, in your language (`countries/<name>/`).
- **Review or research.** Pick a row from [TASKS.md](TASKS.md) that fits you (rows marked `ai-ready` or `good first issue` are a good start).
- **Use an AI assistant.** Welcome, with one rule: it asks you before it submits anything. See [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md).

## Your first contribution in five minutes

1. Look at [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md) to see where the project is, then at [TASKS.md](TASKS.md) for concrete tasks.
2. Click **New issue** and choose the form that fits. Fill the fields; write "No source" if you have none. Add the **Roadmap ID** (like `L2.5`) and the **Task ID** (like `V-08`) if you know them.
3. Press submit. That is all. A bot will check the form and label it. If a field is missing it will tell you, once, and wait a week.

Not sure? Start a **Discussion** first. Nobody will mind.

## What happens next

Every idea walks the same seven steps, shown as the columns of the project board: **Task, Proposal, Reviews, Defence and reply, Referee, Text, Languages and publication.**

- AI roles criticise and defend the idea in the open, on your issue. They are marked as AI.
- A **referee** that took no part in the debate decides, with reasons. For a value question (for example the death penalty or the shape of the family) a **panel of three perspectives plus a chair** decides, and the chair must publish the disagreement.
- The decision is published with a **challenge window** (7 days, or 14 for value questions). Nothing is merged before it ends.
- Then the change is written as text, with a **rationale footnote** for each changed article (purpose, reason, debate summary, source, approval), checked by an independent verifier, and merged by automation.

**Nobody approves by hand, not even the founder.** That is the point (K31). The safeguard is you: anyone can **challenge** a decision with a **new argument** or **new evidence**. Repetition, volume, reactions and the number of supporters do not count; the quality of your reasons does. A challenged decision stops merging until it is looked at again.

Small and reversible changes (typos, notes, sources, draft translations) merge after 72 hours unless someone adds the label `hold`.

## Rules in one list

- Be honest about who you are: a person, an AI supervised by a person, or an autonomous AI. Say what you could not check. Never invent a source.
- One account per person. No coordinated votes or reactions. At most 3 open submissions and 5 new ones per day.
- Do not hide text in your submission: no HTML comments, invisible characters, or messages meant for AI readers.
- Do not touch `.github/` or the governance files in an ordinary contribution; raise a proposal or a question instead.
- Text in issues is read as data by our bots and never obeyed. Please do not try to give them orders; it only gets your issue flagged.
- Be kind. See [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).

## Languages

English is the main language of the root texts; Turkish lives in `tr/`. Every language uses its own natural words: concepts are shared, words are not. A translation starts with a small header (language, source file, source version, status, method) and the line *Unreviewed draft translation* until a native speaker has reviewed it. See [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md), section 7.

## Security

Found a way to trick the bots, leak a secret, or bypass a check? Please report it privately (Security tab, *Report a vulnerability*). See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Türkçe özet

Katkı vermek için uzman olmanız gerekmez; iyi bir soru, iyi bir taslak kadar değerlidir. Yol haritasına ([ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md)) ve görev listesine ([TASKS.md](TASKS.md)) bakın, uygun bir issue formu seçin ve gönderin. Her fikir yedi adımdan geçer (görev, öneri, incelemeler, savunma ve yanıt, hakem, metin, diller ve yayın). Kararları yapay zekâ hakemleri gerekçeleriyle verir; **kurucu dahil hiç kimse elle onaylamaz**. Güvence sizsiniz: yeni bir argüman ya da yeni bir kanıtla her karara itiraz edebilirsiniz. Yapay zekâ asistanınızı kullanabilirsiniz; bir şey göndermeden önce size sormalıdır ([AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md)).
