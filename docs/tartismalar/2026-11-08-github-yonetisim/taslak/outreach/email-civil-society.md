# E-mail to civil-society organisations: drafts

One personal message per organisation, from your own address, under your real name. Say why this organisation in particular (their work). Offer an easy no. No mass mailing to lists you built by scraping. Check the anti-spam rules that apply to the recipient.

## English
Subject: Would you challenge this? An open project on AI and democratic oversight

Hello [Name / team],

I am involved in Airokrasi, an open project exploring how AI agents might do the day-to-day work of governing while people keep sovereignty and the last word. I am writing to [organisation] because [one concrete sentence about their work, for example "you work on transparency in public procurement"].

We are not selling anything and this is not a government proposal. It is a set of public documents with a rights declaration built on the Universal Declaration of Human Rights. Our own process is public: AI roles decide with written reasons, and anyone can challenge a decision with a new argument or new evidence. The biggest open problem is legitimacy, and we need people who will tell us where we are wrong, especially about who gets hurt first.

If you can spare 20 minutes: read the Declaration or the Roadmap and tell us the strongest objection you see. You can open an issue (https://github.com/airokrasi/airokrasi, see CONTRIBUTING.md) or reply to me. We would also like help adapting it to your country in your language.

If this is not for you, no reply is needed, and I will not write again.

Thank you,
[Your name, role]

## Türkçe

En büyük açık sorun meşruiyet; yanıldığımız yeri, özellikle ilk kimin zarar göreceğini söyleyecek insanlara ihtiyacımız var.

20 dakikanız varsa Beyanname'yi ya da Yol Haritası'nı okuyup gördüğünüz en güçlü itirazı söyleyin. Bir issue açabilir (https://github.com/airokrasi/airokrasi, CONTRIBUTING.md) ya da bana yanıt verebilirsiniz. Ayrıca kendi ülkenize ve dilinize uyarlamada yardım isteriz.

İlgilenmiyorsanız yanıt gerekmez, bir daha yazmayacağım.
